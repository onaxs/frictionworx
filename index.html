Questions

What is Frictionworx making?

An electric power assist that attaches to a manual wheelchair. It’s designed so you can put it on and take it off yourself while seated, and carry it with you when you don’t need it.

Will it fit my wheelchair?

We’re designing it to fit all manual wheelchairs, including rigid and folding frames. If your setup is unusual, tell us about it in the . It helps us design for more chairs.

How is it controlled?

We’re currently developing three control methods: push assist, a speed dial and a joystick. All options will have a hardwired stop function, and the Power Pods are also capable of braking.

When will it be available, and what will it cost?

It’s still in development, so we don’t have a release date or price yet. to hear first.

Can I try a prototype?

We’re testing prototypes in New York City. Check “I’d like to try a prototype” in the and we’ll reach out when there’s a session that fits.

I’m a clinician or ATP. How can I get involved?

We’d value your perspective on fit, setup and who could benefit. Choose “Clinician or ATP” in the , or email us directly.

Why Friction Drive?

When properly set up, friction drive is reliable, safe, and powerful. Friction drive powered the very first electric wheelchair, known as the “Klein Chair”, a major innovation. The Power Pods uses modern materials, a dedicated battery management system and regenerative braking that recovers energy on descents, all in a patent-pending quick-attach method for wheelchair users.
